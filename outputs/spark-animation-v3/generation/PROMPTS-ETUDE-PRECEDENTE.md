## Étude des poses

Use case: logo-brand / precise-object-edit.
Asset type: a professional 2D animation in-between study sheet for the existing Spark symbol.
Input image: the supplied white-and-orange mark on dark navy is the exact identity master, not a loose style reference. Preserve its specific blade, four-point concave star and thin crossed rays. The three orange shapes are flames, not leaves.

Create ONE clean 3-column by 2-row production contact sheet, six equally sized square cels, read left to right then top to bottom. Request wide 3:2 canvas, preferably 3072x2048. No lettering, no panel numbers, no grid lines, no arrows, no borders. All six cels have the same flat #0A0E1A background and identical fixed registration, camera, logo scale, white silhouette and white geometry. Give every cel generous identical margins. The exact original white blade, star and rays should be unchanged in ALL six frames; the white structure acts as the registration reference.

Animate ONLY the orange flame silhouettes through ONE restrained, natural rising wave. Three continuous orange shapes in every cel, positioned in the same three areas as the master. The large left flame passes in front of the white diagonal ray; upper-right and lower-right flames stay behind the white. Keep each lower attachment/root absolutely fixed in all six cels. Preserve clear concave spaces and separation between flames.
Cel 1: faithful master resting flame shapes.
Cel 2: the lower bodies slightly gather inward, while tips trail naturally.
Cel 3: a soft S-shaped bend travels upward through the flame bodies, creating a flowing stretch; tips remain connected and taper.
Cel 4: the wave reaches the upper thirds; tips elongate slightly with beautiful continuous arcs, mass and clear silhouette.
Cel 5: the bodies relax while tips finish the same motion, with less stretch.
Cel 6: near the original resting shapes, continuing the return to cel 1.

Art direction: Japanese hand-drawn 2D effects animation translated into refined flat graphic shapes. The meaning is inner passion, a brief life burning with purpose, freedom and helping others surpass themselves. Quietly alive, intentional, not aggressive. Each flame is a single elegant continuous mass with a stable root, a soft full belly and a naturally tapered tip. A small amount of asymmetry gives life. Limit each contour to a few beautiful broad C/S curves. The wave travels rather than wobbling every point. Flat exact flame orange #F05A28, pure white, navy only. No gradient, outline, texture, lighting, 3D, smoke, particles, sparks, droplets, extra fragments, split tongues, serrations, jagged edges, shards, fractal shapes, elastic white geometry or decorative swirls. Do not redesign the logo. Make these drawings plausible consecutive cels, not six independent logo alternatives. Focus on extremely clean, coherent flame drawing and visual continuity.
