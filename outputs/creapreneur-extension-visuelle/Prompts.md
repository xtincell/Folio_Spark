# Prompts de génération

Les trois familles ont été produites avec le générateur d’images natif.

## 01 · Objets sous pression

```text
Create original visual assets for the French-language podcast Créapreneur, about the real work of creative entrepreneurship in francophone Africa. Make ONE square production contact sheet made of exactly FOUR full-bleed SQUARE tiles in a strict equal 2 x 2 grid. Seam divisions at exactly the midpoint in both directions, no gutters, no border, no external sheet title. Each tile will be cropped and used independently as imagery in video or on a poster. Keep the valuable forms within each tile's own 9% safe area. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no numerals. Visual color system from the supplied reference boards described here: vivid fiery red-orange #FF672D, warm paper white #F3EFE7, nearly black #0A0A0A and graphite. Use those colors, believable values in between, strong graphic contrast, and powerful editorial economy. Avoid stock illustration clichés and generic podcast motifs.

01. Photographic object metaphors. Four original, witty, art-directed studio photographs of real-feeling sculptural props. Memorable compositions with sizable silhouettes, analog 35mm grain, controlled hard side light, precise shadows, luxurious tactile details. TOP LEFT on ivory background: a giant vermilion binder clip physically choking a many-layered stack of stiff cream estimate paper, which bows under the pressure; close three-quarter shot. TOP RIGHT on black background: a long bright orange pencil tied into an impossible knot in its middle, tip clearly sharpened, oriented diagonally as a long back-and-forth route turned into an obstacle; graphic high contrast. BOTTOM LEFT on orange background: a stack of four black sculptural hats of visibly different architectural silhouettes, perfectly centered as a balancing act, matt felt and strong asymmetric shadows; all hats should read as purposeful headwear, like a creative doing four jobs. BOTTOM RIGHT on warm grey ivory: a single shiny black domino at the beginning of its fall facing a large wall of tightly packed cream dominoes, one orange domino at the point of contact, bold diagonal perspective, about cascading project consequences. No cell contains any element from another cell. Cut-friendly; subjects substantially separated from edges. Studio photography and practical material experiments, not flat symbols.
```

## 02 · Scènes du métier

```text
Create original visual assets for the French-language podcast Créapreneur, about the real work of creative entrepreneurship in francophone Africa. Make ONE square production contact sheet made of exactly FOUR full-bleed SQUARE tiles in a strict equal 2 x 2 grid. Seam divisions at exactly the midpoint in both directions, no gutters, no border, no external sheet title. Each tile will be cropped and used independently as imagery in video or on a poster. Keep the valuable forms within each tile's own 9% safe area. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no numerals. Visual color system from the supplied reference boards described here: vivid fiery red-orange #FF672D, warm paper white #F3EFE7, nearly black #0A0A0A and graphite. Use those colors, believable values in between, strong graphic contrast, and powerful editorial economy. Avoid stock illustration clichés and generic podcast motifs.

02. Four single-panel editorial narrative illustrations, a bold graphic novel spun out of a creative's real working day. Manual relief-print aesthetic, linocut plus two-pass orange overprint on cream, heavy angular silhouettes and expressive asymmetric faces, rich carved hatching, never faux-vintage brown. Contemporary Black West/Central African characters in urban creative spaces, inventive compositions with different camera angles, ordinary clothes, professional environment, no caricature or stereotyping. TOP LEFT: two creative collaborators at opposite ends of a small table, each holding a deliberately different visual sketch of the same project, clearly discovering that they briefed different things. Oblique angle, black foreground silhouette at left, orange background plane. TOP RIGHT: hands of a Black creative pulling one approved sheet from a wall crowded with crossed-out composition thumbnails, single orange sheet against warm paper and hard black contours; over-the-shoulder crop with enough context. BOTTOM LEFT: night, one Black producer in profile marks a planned deadline on an unlettered wall grid while a coworker with a second shape in hand points further down the schedule, city window behind, black ink dominant, orange accent. BOTTOM RIGHT: Black designer slides a finished orange layout across the desk to a client, client hand stops the paper with two fingers, graphic overhead shot with bodies/arms cropping at tile borders. Crisp economical printmaking. No lettering or pseudo-lettering even on sketches, focus on simple shapes. Each composition self-contained.
```

## 07 · Coulisses du plateau

```text
Outil natif ImageGen. Sortie découpée en quatre cadrages de 720 × 540 px.

Generate one landscape 4:3 photographic contact sheet of exactly FOUR separate finished B-roll frames, with the four equal-size 4:3 frames meeting at exactly the vertical and horizontal midlines in a 2 columns × 2 rows grid. Absolutely no gutters, no empty padding at sheet edges, no captions, no camera UI, no readable text, and no logos. Each frame must work as a standalone photograph when quartered. Art direction: an independent francophone West/Central African creative entrepreneurship podcast recorded in a small real studio, documentary authenticity, sparse modest set. Professional high-contrast editorial BLACK-AND-WARM PAPER WHITE analogue 35mm photography, near-black #111111, light highlights #F2EEE6, subtle film grain, accented with a SINGLE object or glow in vivid vermilion #FF682B in each frame (selective color). Clear contrast and visible visual story in each quarter. Top left frame: macro photo of a Black adult's two hands, one steadying a warm off-white printed estimate with illegible abstract short lines and the other precisely crossing out one line with an orange broad-tipped china marker, rich directional light cutting across warm tabletop. No face. Top right frame: extreme close-up of large knurled audio mixer dial, a cable forms a dramatic diagonal sweep across the black control surface, orange glow in one LED bank, crisp foreground, shallow depth of field. Bottom left frame: WIDE cinematic documentary portrait of an empty black textile guest chair on the right and side-address podcast mic on boom in silhouette on the left, grey plaster wall, rectangular orange light strip bleeding from the rear left, a real interior with strong dark/light planes, main chair clear despite chiaroscuro. Bottom right frame: top-down view of director's workspace before a recording, an open notebook with rectangles, arrows and rough storyboard thumbnail marks, cafe-black headphones resting on right of notebook and angular vermilion gaffer tape on corner, tangible paper fibers and angled shadows. Strong and different compositions, a consistent sparse gritty design, overall disciplined black warm white and orange. Do not attempt to depict any real person. Full bleed each photograph, grid exactly at horizontal and vertical center.
```
